  UiLabel inherits UiView.
</Note>

## Properties
### `Text: string`
The text displayed by the label.

### `TextSize: number`
The size of the text in pixels.

### `TextColor: Color3`
The color of the text.

### `TextStroke: number`
The thickness of the text's outline.

### `TextStrokeColor: Color3`
The color of the text's outline.

### `TextWrapped: boolean`
Whether the text wraps onto multiple lines when it exceeds the label's width.

### `FontWeight: FontWeight`
The weight of the font. See the [FontWeight](/enums/fontweight) enum for the available values.
