<Note iconType="regular">
  UiSurface inherits DynamicInstance.
</Note>

A surface in the 3D world that renders UI. Any
[UiInstance](/abstract-classes/uiinstance) parented to a `UiSurface` is drawn on
its canvas.

## Properties
### `CanvasSize: Vector2`
The size of the surface's canvas in pixels. UI elements are positioned within
this area. Example:
```lua
script.Parent.CanvasSize = Vector2.new(800, 600)
```

### `Shaded: boolean`
Whether the surface is affected by lighting.

### `DoubleSided: boolean`
Whether the surface renders on both sides.

### `Billboard: BillboardMode`
How the surface rotates to face the camera. See the
[BillboardMode](/enums/billboardmode) enum for the available values.

### `Disabled: boolean`
Whether the surface stops rendering its UI.
