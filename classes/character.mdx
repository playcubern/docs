  Character inherits DynamicInstance.
</Note>

Represents a [Player](/classes/player)'s avatar in the 3D world. It controls the
appearance of the avatar, including which body parts and accessories are worn
and their colors. Access it through a player's
[`Character`](/classes/player) property. In addition to everything inherited from
[DynamicInstance](/abstract-classes/dynamicinstance), it has the following members.

## Properties
### `HeadId: number`
The identifier of the head worn by the character.

### `TorsoId: number`
The identifier of the torso worn by the character.

### `FaceId: number`
The identifier of the face worn by the character.

### `ShirtId: number`
The identifier of the shirt worn by the character.

### `Hat1: number`
The identifier of the character's first hat accessory.

### `Hat2: number`
The identifier of the character's second hat accessory.

### `Hat3: number`
The identifier of the character's third hat accessory.

### `Hat4: number`
The identifier of the character's fourth hat accessory.

### `HeadColor: Color3`
The color of the character's head. Example:
```lua
game.Players.LocalPlayer.Character.HeadColor = Color3.fromHex("#ffcc99")
```

### `TorsoColor: Color3`
The color of the character's torso.

### `RightArmColor: Color3`
The color of the character's right arm.

### `LeftArmColor: Color3`
The color of the character's left arm.

### `RightLegColor: Color3`
The color of the character's right leg.

### `LeftLegColor: Color3`
The color of the character's left leg.
