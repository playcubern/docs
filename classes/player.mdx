Represents a player in the game. In addition to everything inherited from [DynamicInstance](/abstract-classes/dynamicinstance), it has the following members.

## Properties
### `Username: string`
The username of the player.

### `UserId: number`
The unique identifier of the player's account.

### `AnimationState: number`
The current animation state of the player.
