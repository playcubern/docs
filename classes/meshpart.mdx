<Note iconType="regular">
  MeshPart inherits DynamicInstance.
</Note>

A part whose visible shape comes from a custom mesh instead of a primitive
[PartShape](/enums/partshape). It has no additional properties, methods or
events beyond those inherited from [DynamicInstance](/abstract-classes/dynamicinstance).
