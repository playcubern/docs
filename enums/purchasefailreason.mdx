The `PurchaseFailReason` enum describes why a purchase made through the
[MarketService](/services/marketservice) did not go through. Each value is an
[EnumItem](/types/enumitem) accessed through `Enum.PurchaseFailReason`.

## Values
### `Enum.PurchaseFailReason.None`
The purchase did not fail.

### `Enum.PurchaseFailReason.Declined`
The player declined the purchase prompt.

### `Enum.PurchaseFailReason.InsufficientFunds`
The player does not have enough funds to buy the product.

### `Enum.PurchaseFailReason.NotForSale`
The product is not for sale.

### `Enum.PurchaseFailReason.AlreadyOwned`
The player already owns the product.

### `Enum.PurchaseFailReason.Error`
An internal error occurred while processing the purchase.

---

## Methods
### `GetEnumItems(): { PurchaseFailReason }`
Returns a table of every `PurchaseFailReason` value. Example:
```lua
for _, item in Enum.PurchaseFailReason:GetEnumItems() do
    print(item.Name)
end
```
