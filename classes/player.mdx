Represents a player in the game. In addition to everything inherited from [Instance](/abstract-classes/instance), it has the following members.

## Properties
### `Username: string`
The username of the player.

### `UserId: number`
The unique identifier of the player's account.

### `AnimationState: number`
The current animation state of the player.

### `Character: Character`
The [Character](/classes/character) that represents the player's avatar in the
3D world. Example:
```lua
local character = game.Players.LocalPlayer.Character
character.HeadColor = Color3.fromHex("#ff0000")
```
