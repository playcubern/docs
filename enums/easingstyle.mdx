The `EasingStyle` enum defines the curve a [Tween](/classes/tween) follows as it
moves from its start value to its goal. Each value is an
[EnumItem](/types/enumitem) accessed through `Enum.EasingStyle` and used by
[TweenInfo](/types/tweeninfo).

## Values
### `Enum.EasingStyle.Linear`
Moves at a constant speed.

### `Enum.EasingStyle.Sine`
Gentle easing based on a sine wave.

### `Enum.EasingStyle.Back`
Slightly overshoots past the start or goal before settling.

### `Enum.EasingStyle.Quad`
Quadratic easing.

### `Enum.EasingStyle.Cubic`
Cubic easing, stronger than `Quad`.

### `Enum.EasingStyle.Quart`
Quartic easing, stronger than `Cubic`.

### `Enum.EasingStyle.Quint`
Quintic easing, stronger than `Quart`.

### `Enum.EasingStyle.Exponential`
Very sharp easing based on an exponential curve.

### `Enum.EasingStyle.Circular`
Easing based on a quarter circle.

### `Enum.EasingStyle.Bounce`
Bounces against the goal like a dropped ball.

### `Enum.EasingStyle.Elastic`
Springs back and forth around the goal like a rubber band.

---

## Methods
### `GetEnumItems(): { EasingStyle }`
Returns a table of every `EasingStyle` value. Example:
```lua
for _, item in Enum.EasingStyle:GetEnumItems() do
    print(item.Name)
end
```
