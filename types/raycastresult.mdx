A `RaycastResult` describes what a ray hit. It is returned by
[Workspace's](/services/workspace) `Raycast` method.

## Properties
### `Instance: DynamicInstance`
The object that was hit by the ray.

### `Position: Vector3`
The world position where the ray hit the object.

### `Normal: Vector3`
The surface normal at the point of impact.

### `Distance: number`
The distance from the ray's origin to the hit position.
