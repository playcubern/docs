The SoundService holds settings that apply to all sound in the game.

## Properties
### `MasterVolume: number`
The overall volume of the game, between `0` and `1`. Setting a value outside
that range raises an error. Example:
```lua
game:GetService("SoundService").MasterVolume = 0.5
```
