A non-player character. An Npc holds a [Character](/classes/character), which
is the body that appears and moves in the world, and can be controlled by
scripts with the Character's `Move`, `MoveTo` and `Jump` methods.

The Npc's `Position`, `Rotation` and `Scale` are those of its Character, so
setting them moves the Character. An Npc is always anchored and never collides
or fires touch events itself; its Character handles physics instead.

## Properties
### `Character: Character`
The [Character](/classes/character) that makes up the Npc's body. This property
is read-only. Example:
```lua
local npc = workspace.Npc

npc.Character.WalkSpeed = 10
npc.Character:MoveTo(Vector3.new(20, 0, 20))
```
