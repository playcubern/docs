  BodyPart inherits DynamicInstance.
</Note>

A physical part that makes up a [Character](/classes/character)'s body, such as
a head, torso, arm or leg. A character's body parts are its children named
`Head`, `Torso`, `LeftArm`, `RightArm`, `LeftLeg` and `RightLeg`. In addition
to everything inherited from [DynamicInstance](/abstract-classes/dynamicinstance),
it has the following members.

## Properties
### `Color: Color3`
The color of the body part. Changing it fires the character's
`AppearanceChanged` event. Example:
```lua
game.Players.LocalPlayer.Character.Head.Color = Color3.fromHex("#ffcc99")
```
