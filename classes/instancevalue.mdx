<Note iconType="regular">
  InstanceValue inherits ValueBase.
</Note>

A value instance that holds a reference to another [Instance](/abstract-classes/instance) or `nil`. It also has the `Changed` event inherited from [ValueBase](/abstract-classes/valuebase).

## Properties
### `Value: Instance?`
The [Instance](/abstract-classes/instance) stored by the instance, or `nil`.
