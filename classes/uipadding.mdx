---
title: "UiPadding"
description: "Adds space between the edges of its parent and the parent's children"
icon: "expand"
---

<Note iconType="regular">
  UiPadding inherits Instance.
</Note>

UiPadding shrinks the area its parent's children are placed in. Parent it to a
[UiPanel](/classes/uipanel) (or any other UI instance, a
[UiLayer](/classes/uilayer) or a [UiSurface](/classes/uisurface)) and every UI
instance under that same parent is positioned and sized inside the padded area
instead of the full parent:

- A child at `Position` `{0, 0}, {0, 0}` sits at the padded top-left corner.
- A child with `Size` `{1, 0}, {1, 0}` fills the padded area, not the whole
  parent.
- A [UiListLayout](/classes/uilistlayout) under the same parent stacks its
  children inside the padded area.

The scale part of each side is relative to the parent's size: `PaddingLeft`
and `PaddingRight` use its width, `PaddingTop` and `PaddingBottom` use its
height. If a parent has more than one UiPadding, the first one is used.

```lua
local padding = Instance.new("UiPadding", script.Parent)
padding.PaddingTop = UDim.new(0, 12)
padding.PaddingBottom = UDim.new(0, 12)
padding.PaddingLeft = UDim.new(0.05, 0)
padding.PaddingRight = UDim.new(0.05, 0)
```

## Properties
### `PaddingTop: UDim`
The space between the top edge of the parent and its children. Defaults to
`UDim.new(0, 0)`.

### `PaddingBottom: UDim`
The space between the bottom edge of the parent and its children. Defaults to
`UDim.new(0, 0)`.

### `PaddingLeft: UDim`
The space between the left edge of the parent and its children. Defaults to
`UDim.new(0, 0)`.

### `PaddingRight: UDim`
The space between the right edge of the parent and its children. Defaults to
`UDim.new(0, 0)`.
