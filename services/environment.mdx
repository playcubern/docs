The Environment service controls global lighting and atmosphere of the world.

## Properties
### `GlobalShadows: boolean`
Whether global shadows are enabled.

### `Brightness: number`
The overall brightness of the scene.

### `Ambient: Color3`
The ambient light color applied to the whole scene.

### `FogEnabled: boolean`
Whether fog is enabled.

### `FogColor: Color3`
The color of the fog.
