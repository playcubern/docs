The MarketService provides access to purchasable products, letting scripts
check ownership, look up product details and prompt players to buy.

## Methods
### `OwnsItem(productId: number): boolean`
Returns whether the local player owns the product with the given id.

### `PlayerOwns(player: Player, productId: number): boolean`
Returns whether `player` owns the product with the given id.

### `PromptPurchase(productId: number): nil`
Prompts the local player to purchase the product with the given id.

### `PromptPurchase(player: Player, productId: number): nil`
Prompts `player` to purchase the product with the given id.

### `GetProductInfo(productId: number): ProductInfo`
Returns the [ProductInfo](/types/productinfo) for the product with the given
id.

---

## Events
### `PurchaseFinished: Event<Player, number, boolean, PurchaseFailReason>`
Fired when a purchase prompted by `PromptPurchase` completes. Passes the
player who was prompted, the product id, whether the purchase succeeded, and
the reason for failure if it did not.
```lua
local marketService = game:GetService("MarketService")
marketService.PurchaseFinished:Connect(function(player, productId, wasPurchased, failReason)
    if wasPurchased then
        print(player.Name .. " bought product " .. productId)
    end
end)
```
