The Environment service controls global lighting and atmosphere of the world.

## Properties
### `GlobalShadows: boolean`
Whether global shadows are enabled.

### `SunBrightness: number`
The brightness of the sun light in the scene.

### `SunColor: Color3`
The color of the sun light in the scene.

### `BackgroundMode: BackgroundMode`
How the background of the world is rendered. See
[BackgroundMode](/enums/backgroundmode).

### `BackgroundFlatColor: Color3`
The color used for the background when `BackgroundMode` is
`Enum.BackgroundMode.FlatColor`.

### `Ambient: Color3`
The ambient light color applied to the whole scene.

### `FogMode: FogMode`
How fog is rendered. See [FogMode](/enums/fogmode).

### `FogEnabled: boolean`
Whether fog is enabled.

### `FogColor: Color3`
The color of the fog.

### `FogStartDistance: number`
The distance from the camera at which fog begins.

<Note iconType="regular">
  This property only applies when `FogMode` is `Enum.FogMode.Standard`. When
  `FogMode` is `Enum.FogMode.Volumetric`, fog always starts at the camera and
  `FogStartDistance` has no effect.
</Note>

### `FogEndDistance: number`
The distance from the camera at which fog reaches full density.

### `FogDensity: number`
How thick the fog is. Higher values make the fog more opaque. Must be `0` or
greater. The value applies to whichever `FogMode` is currently active.

### `Saturation: number`
The color saturation applied to the whole scene.

### `ActiveSkybox: Skybox`
The [Skybox](/classes/skybox) currently used to render the sky. Changes to the
skybox, such as setting its `SkyPreset`, are applied to the sky right away.
Example:
```lua
local environment = game:GetService("Environment")
environment.ActiveSkybox.SkyPreset = Enum.SkyPreset.Night
```
